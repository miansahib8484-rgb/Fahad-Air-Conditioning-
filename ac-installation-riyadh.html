How Customers Find Us

People shopping for an installation usually start with the unit and end with the installer. They type ac installation, then narrow to size — 1.5 ton split ac, gree 1.5 ton split ac, haier 1.5 ton ac, midea ac 2 ton, air conditioning 1 ton, 2.7 ton ac — before asking whether the room can actually take it. That's where room calculation for ac matters: an undersized unit runs flat out all summer, and an oversized one cycles on and off without drying the air properly.

Type matters too. Wall-mounted units come up as air conditioners for the wall, floor units as stand ac, ceiling units as gree concealed ac, and the part you see indoors as the indoor ac unit. The outdoor half, the gree outdoor unit on many Riyadh villas, needs airflow clearance and a stable mount, which is why we survey before we quote.

Brand-led searches such as carrier ac and carrier air conditioning are common too. Whichever brand you've bought, we install and commission it, and the warranty on the installation is ours.
