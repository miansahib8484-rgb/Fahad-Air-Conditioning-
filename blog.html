How Customers Find Us

The blog is built around the questions we're actually asked. Some are how-to searches — how to fix ac, hvac troubleshooting guide — and some are diagnosis questions such as reasons for air conditioner not cooling, ac why not cooling and ac not blowing cold air.

Noise and water problems come up constantly: air conditioner sounds loud, vibration sound in ac, why water from ac drips onto a balcony, or a fan that stalls — ac fan is not spinning or ac fan speed low or auto.

And then there's the cost side: a c fan motor cost and air conditioner coil replacement cost get searched before people call anyone. Our guides give honest ranges where we can and say 'it depends' when it does.
